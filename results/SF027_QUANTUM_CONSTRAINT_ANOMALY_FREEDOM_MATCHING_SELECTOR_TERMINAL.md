## LANE C — AMBIGUITY / COUNTEREXAMPLE AUDIT

Primary counter-audits:

- R. Gambini, J. Lewandowski, D. Marolf, J. Pullin, `On the consistency of the constraint algebra in spin network quantum gravity`, arXiv:gr-qc/9710018.
- A. Perez, `On the regularization ambiguities in loop quantum gravity`, arXiv:gr-qc/0509118.

Gambini et al. show that the precise sense in which the Hamiltonian-constraint commutator is anomaly-free is nontrivial: a vanishing commutator on a chosen dual/state space can correspond to a trivial realization of the classical structure-function side, while alternative regularizations expose other behavior.

Perez explicitly identifies regularization ambiguities producing an apparently infinite set of possible quantum theories and notes that the 3+1 analysis is incomplete because of the physical-inner-product problem. Some choices can be rejected by stronger consistency controls; that does not turn anomaly freedom alone into a complete selector.

Thus the phrase `anomaly free` has insufficient physical content unless the representation, domain, regularization/ordering equivalence and map to observable matching data are specified.

A comparator existence result, T. Thiemann `Exact quantisation of U(1)^3 quantum gravity via exponentiation of the hypersurface deformation algebroid`, arXiv:2207.08302, demonstrates that exact anomaly-free HDA quantization can be achieved in a related model. It is not the RHPI/ADM Lorentzian gravity matching problem and no content from that model is imported.
