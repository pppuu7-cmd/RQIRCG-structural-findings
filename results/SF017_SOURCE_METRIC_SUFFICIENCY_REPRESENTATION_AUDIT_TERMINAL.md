## Lane A — RBG-to-Einstein mapping

A wide class of Ricci-based metric-affine gravity theories admits a correspondence with Einstein-like equations for an auxiliary metric, with the non-Einstein information transferred into nonlinear relations/interactions in the matter/source sector.

Representative references:

- V. I. Afonso, G. J. Olmo, D. Rubiera-Garcia, *Mapping Ricci-based theories of gravity into general relativity*, arXiv:`1801.10406`;
- A. Delhom, V. Miralles, A. Penuelas, *Effective interactions in Ricci-Based Gravity models below the non-metricity scale*, arXiv:`1907.05615`.

The first establishes a broad solution-space correspondence between RBGs and GR. The second shows that minimally coupled matter in RBG develops nontrivial effective interactions below the nonmetricity scale when represented in the Einstein-like variables.

Thus the distinction

`modified gravity with auxiliary connection`

versus

`Einstein gravity + modified matter/source interactions`

is not invariant under the relevant reformulation.

## Lane B — fixed matter calibration

The induced interactions are physical rather than pure notation: they can affect scattering/source behavior and are constrained phenomenologically. Their coefficients are controlled by the RBG/nonmetricity scale and related theory data.

Fixing ordinary low-energy matter observations does not logically force those coefficients to vanish exactly; it constrains them over the calibrated domain. Unless the complete matter EFT/source law is independently fixed to all relevant orders, the same residual microscopic datum can reappear as a higher-order matter/source operator after moving to the Einstein-like representation.

Therefore the statement “matter is already known” is not a theorem that eliminates the RBG fiber. It would require a prospective matter-sector completeness assumption or sufficiently strong independent calibration at the nonlinear order under test.

Lane classification:

`LOW_ORDER_MATTER_CALIBRATION_CONSTRAINS_BUT_DOES_NOT_GENERICALLY_ELIMINATE_RELOCATED_SOURCE_OPERATORS`.
