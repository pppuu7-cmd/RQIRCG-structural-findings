## Candidate under test

MPC — Minimal Propagating Completion:

> Complete the already justified weak-field carrier without adding new propagating physical degrees of freedom, new independent initial-data order, or a new dimensionful dynamical scale unless such structure is forced by an already-frozen requirement; obtain nonlinearity by closing the interactions of the already-required variables under one local causal generator.

SF002 tests whether this is an independently motivated physical principle or a retrospective bundle chosen because it narrows the theory space.

## A — necessity attribution

### Same weak-field coupling and lower-order recovery

`INTERNALLY_MOTIVATED`.

This is already frozen by the RCG-002 construction history and is not new selector information.

### Local causal/retarded closure

`INTERNALLY_MOTIVATED_AS_CONSISTENCY_REQUIREMENT`, but not enough to fix a unique nonlinear generator.

The construction contract already demands no effective superluminal signalling and the baseline carrier has an explicit retarded formulation. Causality therefore remains a legitimate hard gate.

### No new propagating physical DOF

`ONLY_MINIMALITY_PREFERENCE` under currently frozen authority.

The old programme used a single symmetric rank-2 carrier in the validated linearized baseline, but no frozen result establishes that nonlinear completion is forbidden from introducing additional physical modes. The fact that a smaller field content is attractive is not yet a physical derivation.

### No higher independent initial-data order / no higher derivatives

`NOT_INTERNALLY_ENTAILED`.

G59 proved uniqueness only inside an explicitly frozen local, linear, two-derivative, single-symmetric-tensor class and explicitly warned that the result is not a no-go theorem beyond that class. G60 then prospectively demonstrated a two-dimensional local four-derivative linearized gauge-invariant escape space modulo the 4D Gauss-Bonnet relation. Thus the historical research programme itself treated higher derivative order as an admissible structural escape to be audited, not as forbidden by RQIR.

### No new dimensionful dynamical scale

`ONLY_MINIMALITY_PREFERENCE`.

No frozen RQIRCG result supplied an absolute principle excluding a new physical scale. Introducing one without an independent requirement would be underdetermined, but forbidding every such scale is equally an additional assumption.

## B — bundle audit

The clauses are logically separable.

Examples of structurally distinct possibilities consistent with the inherited weak-field branch include:

1. same field variable and gauge symmetry with higher-derivative invariants;
2. same leading propagator but nonlinear invariants beginning beyond quadratic order;
3. constrained or auxiliary additional variables that do not add propagating modes;
4. additional propagating modes whose effects decouple in the validated weak-field domain;
5. nonlocal effective terms with a causal prescription;
6. new-scale corrections whose coefficients vanish in the inherited limit.

The common slogan “do not multiply dynamical content without necessity” ranks these possibilities by economy but does not physically exclude them. To turn economy into a hard law would itself be the new principle, and no frozen relational requirement currently entails it.

Therefore MPC is a bundle of preferences plus already-existing consistency gates, not yet a discovered physical law.

## C — selector-power audit

Even granting the strongest internally defensible subset — one closed generator, inherited linearized spin-2 gauge structure, causal closure, conserved source, and exact weak-field recovery — nonlinear generator freedom remains.

G59/G60 provide direct internal evidence for the broader point: restriction to two derivatives is selection information. Once that restriction is relaxed, independent gauge-invariant directions appear already at the linearized structural level. At nonlinear order, terms that begin above quadratic order can preserve the same leading propagator while modifying interactions, so preserving the weak-field DOF alone does not generically fix all nonlinear vertices.

Hence the selector cannot be completed by silently upgrading “minimality” into a theorem.

## D — falsification / escape audit

MPC fails the strict gate for two different reasons:

- **justification failure:** the no-new-DOF/no-new-scale/two-derivative clauses are not all inherited consequences;
- **robustness failure:** weakening those clauses to the internally justified subset leaves inequivalent generator classes.

This conclusion does not claim that higher-derivative or extra-mode theories are healthy. Stability, unitarity, causal initial-value formulation, and positive operational reduction can reject many such theories downstream. But those rejection tests are not equivalent to a prior principle that all such directions are forbidden.

## External uniqueness check — quarantined from the verdict

Known massless spin-2 consistency results become very restrictive after assumptions such as locality, fixed field content, and bounded derivative order are imposed. This is fully compatible with the SF002 result: the mathematical rigidity is conditional on precisely the assumptions that SF002 is not allowed to smuggle in as unexplained physics.

## Terminal decision

No generator class is selected.

## NEW STRUCTURAL FINDING

The nonlinear ambiguity cannot be removed merely by combining:

`single closed generator + generic minimality`.

The missing selector must do more than express economy. It must arise from a physical consistency principle that acts directly on the nonlinear interaction structure.

The strongest internally motivated unused structure is the **gauge/relational redundancy itself**. The validated carrier already has a linear gauge redundancy and conserved-source compatibility; RQIR also demands relational/gauge-invariant observables. This suggests the next independent question:

> Does requiring the nonlinear interaction to be a consistent deformation/closure of the inherited gauge redundancy, with the physical gauge-orbit content preserved, uniquely determine the nonlinear generator — or do higher-order gauge-invariant vertices survive?

This question does not presuppose two derivative order, a GR action, or a connected outcome.
