## Formal CUC requirements

A surviving completion must satisfy all four frozen clauses.

### C1 — one closed generator

There is one variational/dynamical object

`Gamma_total[carrier, matter, apparatus/support]`

from which source equations and carrier equations descend. No independent phenomenological source-to-field map may be attached afterward.

### C2 — universal weak-field normalization

All sectors couple to the same carrier with the same inherited weak-field normalization. There is no species-dependent gravitational coupling and no separate coupling constant for apparatus/support or carrier self-energy.

This does **not** stipulate a derivative order or forbid separately invariant carrier terms.

### C3 — source closure

The source entering the carrier equation is the total source generated by the same closed generator, including matter, apparatus/support and the carrier contribution in whatever covariant/constraint-compatible form the completion itself defines.

### C4 — associative closed composition

For three closed subsystems `A,B,C`, regrouping is physically immaterial:

`(A ⊕ B) ⊕ C  ~  A ⊕ (B ⊕ C)`.

Here `~` means the same total generator/equations/physical predictions after the same gauge/relational quotient. The clause excludes grouping-dependent interaction rules and ad hoc pairwise redefinitions.

It does not assume that the generator is quadratic, two-derivative, or free of intrinsic carrier self-interactions.
