## Context

## Scientific question

Is “exactly the inherited two local physical gravitational DOF on generic backgrounds” sufficient to select Einstein-like nonlinear dynamics once one allows mathematically different but physically two-DOF formulations?

## Frozen escape classes

Audit independently:

A. local pure-metric finite-jet theories beyond the `L(g,∂g,∂²g)` class;

B. local metric-affine / auxiliary-field formulations in which extra variables carry no additional propagating gravitational DOF;

C. nonlocal/infinite-derivative formulations engineered to introduce no additional propagator poles;

D. reduced-covariance/minimally-modified two-DOF theories (control class; expected to fail the inherited full spacetime-diffeomorphism requirement).

## Selector criterion

GDP remains sufficient only if every physically admissible escape either:

1. is physically equivalent to the Einstein-Hilbert class in the relevant source/interface domain;
2. introduces extra physical gravitational DOF somewhere in the declared domain;
3. violates an already-frozen independent RQIRCG condition;
4. or requires a genuinely new principle beyond GDP that is explicitly identified.

## Counterexample-first failure criterion

GDP is not a complete selector beyond metric-only scope if there exists an inequivalent completion with:

- exactly two local propagating gravitational DOF on its generic physical branch;
- full spacetime diffeomorphism invariance (for the decisive counterexample; reduced-covariance examples are controls only);
- the inherited weak-field massless spin-2/Newton limit;
- a continuous nonlinear coupling/function not fixed by the inherited calibration;
- different nonlinear source/geometry dynamics;
- no extra propagating gravitational mode merely hidden as an auxiliary variable.

Failure verdict:

`GDP_INSUFFICIENT_BEYOND_METRIC_ONLY_REPRESENTATION_SCOPED`.
