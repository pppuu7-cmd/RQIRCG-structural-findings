## Scientific question

Does fundamental closure / normalized quantum evolution / causal Hamiltonian evolution entail the specific reversibility condition needed by HKT geometrodynamical uniqueness?

## Frozen distinctions

SF019 must not conflate:

1. **invertibility/unitarity** — evolution has an inverse;
2. **microscopic time-reversal symmetry** — the same law is invariant under reversal of time orientation with the appropriate transformation of canonical data;
3. **HKT reversibility** — the reversed spacetime/geometrodynamical history is generated by the same super-Hamiltonian/supermomentum representation;
4. **retarded boundary conditions** — a state/preparation choice selecting causal response, which can break time-reversal at the solution level while the local law remains reversible.
