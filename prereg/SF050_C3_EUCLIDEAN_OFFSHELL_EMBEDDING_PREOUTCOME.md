## Gate

`SF050_C3_EUCLIDEAN_OFFSHELL_EMBEDDING_PREOUTCOME_GATE`

## Question

Can the physical on-shell C3 helicity projector constructed in SF049 be embedded into the Euclidean/off-shell dynamical graviton vertex formalism used by current FRG calculations without silently identifying an off-shell tensor coefficient with the physical on-shell matching coefficient?

## Frozen hypothesis

A useful embedding requires two logically separate objects:

1. a concrete flat-background Euclidean tensor template generated by the cubic expansion of `S_C3 = integral sqrt(g) C C C`;
2. a projection/quotient rule that prevents off-shell Ricci/EOM-redundant six-derivative structures from being mistaken for the physical C3 coefficient.

The existence of a tensor template alone is insufficient for physical matching.

## Frozen derivation target

D1. Expand around flat space `g=delta+h`. Determine the leading cubic `O(h^3)` contribution of `S_C3` and its derivative order.

D2. Define the cubic momentum-space tensor template

`T_C3^(3)(p1,p2,p3) = delta^3 S_C3 / delta h(p1) delta h(p2) delta h(p3) |_{h=0}`.

D3. Determine its linearized gauge/TT properties and its relation under analytic continuation to the SF049 all-plus/all-minus physical projector.

D4. Audit whether one real Euclidean symmetric-point contraction onto `T_C3^(3)` uniquely determines the physical essential C3 coordinate, or whether off-shell six-derivative Ricci/EOM structures contaminate the same tensor space.

D5. Audit the actual tensor ansatz used in modern dynamical-vertex/effective-action reconstruction and determine whether higher-curvature tensor structures are explicitly projected or absorbed into momentum-dependent lower-curvature dressings.

D6. State the minimum additional object required for an FRG flow coefficient that can later be compared with SF049/SF025.

## Frozen controls

C1. Cubic-order control: because `C[g=delta]=0`, any contribution from `sqrt(g)-1` or from one nonlinear Weyl tensor must be power-counted explicitly before entering `O(h^3)`.

C2. Derivative counting: the target cubic local structure must be homogeneous of degree six in external momenta.

C3. Einstein negative control: the physical all-plus/all-minus `p^6` projector must vanish on the Einstein-Hilbert three-point amplitude.

C4. Off-shell redundancy control: a Ricci-containing six-derivative operator that vanishes on Ricci-flat on-shell external gravitons but is nonzero for generic Euclidean off-shell TT legs is sufficient to disprove any claim that a single naive Euclidean C3 contraction is automatically a physical matching projector.

C5. No background/fluctuation equality may be assumed.

C6. No value of the SF043 truncation constant may be fitted or imported into the projection.

## Decision rule

### PASS_EMBEDDING_SCOPED

Requires an explicit Euclidean tensor template plus a projection/quotient construction that uniquely isolates the physical C3 direction from all relevant off-shell six-derivative redundancies in the declared vertex space, with a demonstrated continuation to the SF049 helicity projector.

### PARTIAL_TEMPLATE_ONLY

Use if the C3 tensor template is constructible and its physical continuation is clear, but a unique off-shell physical projector requires a larger six-derivative tensor basis / EOM quotient / field-redefinition map not yet implemented.

### BLOCKED

Use if even the explicit C3 tensor template or its relation to the current dynamical vertex object cannot be defined from available authority.

### FAIL

Use only for an actual inconsistency between the C3 local operator and the SF049 physical amplitude direction.
