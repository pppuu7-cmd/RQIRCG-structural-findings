# SF002 — generator-class selection pre-outcome gate — PREREGISTRATION

Date: 2026-09-14
Status: **PROSPECTIVE / PRE-OUTCOME**

## Why this gate exists

SF001 found that a single closed dynamical generator can unify the old nonlinear-source and positive-operational-map interfaces, but does not by itself uniquely select the nonlinear generator. The remaining freedom concerns which nonlinear generator classes are physically admissible.

No connected three-source prediction has been inspected. `chi_ABC` remains embargoed.

## Question

Can the inherited relational/closed-system architecture independently justify a **minimal propagating completion** principle, rather than introducing restrictions merely because they are known to favor a familiar nonlinear theory?

## Candidate principle MPC

**Minimal Propagating Completion (MPC):**

> When the already validated weak-field carrier and closed source/apparatus system admit a nonlinear completion, the completion may not introduce additional propagating physical degrees of freedom, additional independent initial-data order, or a new dimensionful dynamical scale unless such new structure is forced by an already-frozen RQIR observable or consistency requirement. Nonlinearity must instead arise by closing the interactions of the already-required dynamical variables under one local causal generator.

This is a proposed principle, not an accepted premise.

## Why MPC is not automatically allowed

Each clause could hide a separate assumption. The gate therefore tests whether they follow from one defensible idea — **do not multiply physical dynamical content beyond what the operational requirements force** — or whether they are an illicit conjunction selected to engineer uniqueness.

External GR/spin-2 uniqueness results are quarantined from the selection decision. They may be consulted only after the internal motivation verdict is frozen.

## Frozen tests

### A — necessity attribution
For each MPC clause, identify a frozen internal reason:

- same propagating DOF;
- no higher independent initial-data order;
- no unexplained new dynamical scale;
- local causal closure;
- same weak-field coupling/recovery.

Verdict per clause: `INTERNALLY_MOTIVATED`, `ONLY_MINIMALITY_PREFERENCE`, or `EXTERNAL_THEORY_MOTIVATED`.

MPC fails if any clause essential to selector power is only external-theory motivated.

### B — independence / bundle audit
Attempt to separate the clauses. Construct candidate completion classes that violate one clause while satisfying the others and all inherited lower-order facts. Determine whether the common principle “no unforced dynamical content” really excludes them or whether extra axioms are needed.

### C — selector-power audit
Without calculating `chi_ABC`, determine whether:

`single closed generator + MPC + inherited linear spin-2/gauge/source structure`

leaves more than one inequivalent healthy local nonlinear generator class after field redefinitions and boundary/topological terms are quotiented.

A literature uniqueness theorem may be used only as an external check after the internal admissibility assumptions are explicitly matched one by one.

### D — falsification and escape audit
Search for admissible counterexamples:

- same propagating DOF but higher-derivative-looking formulation reducible by degeneracy/constraints;
- extra nondynamical auxiliary variables;
- topological/boundary terms;
- new interactions without a new scale;
- nonlocal but causal effective completion;
- local completion with the same weak-field limit but inequivalent nonlinear behavior.

The objective is to falsify MPC selector power, not protect it.

## Decision rule

`MPC_SELECTED_FOR_DERIVATION` only if:

1. all selector-essential clauses are internally motivated without outcome or external-theory leakage;
2. they form one coherent principle rather than an arbitrary bundle;
3. the resulting generator class is unique enough to derive nonlinear dynamics without a free connected coefficient;
4. locality/causality and constraint propagation remain derivable obligations, not asserted labels.

Otherwise record either:

- `MPC_NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_JUSTIFIED`, or
- `MPC_INSUFFICIENT_GENERATOR_NONUNIQUENESS_SURVIVES`.

Even a PASS does not authorize novelty claims. It authorizes derivation of the nonlinear equations and physical operational map. Only after those close prospectively may `chi_ABC` be evaluated.
