# SF001 — minimal new physical principle search — PREREGISTRATION

Date: 2026-09-14
Status: **PROSPECTIVE / PRE-OUTCOME**

## Purpose

Search for the minimal independently motivated physical principle that removes the nonlinear underdetermination proven in RQIRCG, without importing a preferred nonlinear theory and without inspecting or tuning the connected observable `chi_ABC`.

## Frozen starting facts

The following are treated as established inputs only:

- operational controlled-phase architecture RCG-002;
- connected three-source observable form `chi_ABC = Delta_A Delta_B Delta_C phi`;
- pairwise/quadratic contributions are annihilated by the third finite difference;
- G97 supplies a closed probe+apparatus/support preparation with exact total momentum bookkeeping in its scoped mechanical model;
- NP1 shows a continuum of connected phase/dephasing extensions survives normalized CPTP evolution, exact lower-face recovery, lower-body phase quotienting, inherited total-translation compatibility, and finite-time evolution;
- CM1 shows that explicit closed sequential mediation and exact mediator reset still do not select the connected phase;
- VB1/V2P1 history shows that current RQIRCG principles do not entail nonlinear dynamics, and that a naive self-coupling proposal remained incomplete at source-map and positive-operational-map interfaces.

No post-VB1 principle candidate is inherited as true physics.

## Outcome embargo

Until SF001 selects a principle, the following are forbidden:

1. computing `chi_ABC` from a proposed new nonlinear model;
2. choosing a nonzero sign/magnitude of `chi_ABC`;
3. inserting a free connected coefficient (`lambda`, `gamma`, `K_3`, cubic vertex coefficient, etc.);
4. importing Einstein equations, an Einstein-Hilbert action, QGR/MSQGR dynamics, semiclassical gravity, stochastic gravity, or gravitational EFT as the model;
5. weakening selection criteria after inspecting candidate consequences.

External literature may be used only as a consistency/uniqueness cross-check after a principle is formulated from the internal starting structure.

## Search space

Candidate principles must be *principles*, not detailed equations chosen for desired output. The initial independent classes are:

### P-A — Closed variational generator / universal dynamical closure
There exists one local generator for the complete closed source+carrier system. The source, apparatus/support, and carrier contribution are obtained from the same generator, so the carrier responds to the total dynamical stress/constraint content generated by that closed system. No independent nonlinear coupling is introduced beyond the already calibrated weak-field coupling and constants required by the inherited lower-order branch.

### P-B — Constraint-preserving self-source recursion
The linearized massless spin-2 carrier must couple iteratively to the conserved stress/constraint contribution generated by its own interaction, with closure required by propagation of the inherited gauge constraint. The iteration is accepted only if it defines a unique local nonlinear completion without an extra coefficient.

### P-C — Closed-history influence principle
For each closed G97 history, the physical carrier and source system admits one normalized causal closed-time-path/influence object generated by the same microscopic dynamics. Higher connected response is not independently specifiable; it must be derived from that generator and its state. No independent higher cumulant functional is permitted.

### P-D — Relational extremality/minimality selector
Among all nonlinear completions satisfying inherited symmetries and lower-order recovery, choose one by an extremality/minimality criterion constructed solely from already-authoritative relational data.

P-D is included as an adversarial class because it risks replacing a free connected coefficient with a free objective/metric.

## Mandatory criteria

A candidate principle can survive SF001 only if all of M1–M8 are PASS at principle level, and M9–M10 are at least `DEFINED_DERIVATION_ROUTE`.

M1. **Internal motivation** — motivated by the closed relational/source architecture or inherited consistency requirements, not by the desired connected signal.

M2. **Physical DOF** — identifies the physical carrier/state variables to which the rule applies.

M3. **Closed source/history map** — supplies a non-arbitrary route from the G97 closed preparation to the dynamical source/history object used by the nonlinear theory, including support/apparatus contributions where required.

M4. **Genuine nonlinear generator** — contains information beyond the Gaussian/quadratic baseline and does not merely constrain an otherwise free channel.

M5. **No new connected datum** — no independent parameter or functional is introduced whose sole role is to set a three-source connected term.

M6. **Conservation/constraint closure** — the same-realization nonlinear conservation/Bianchi/gauge-constraint problem is either solved by the principle or uniquely delegated to a derivation that can falsify the candidate.

M7. **Causal/retarded meaning** — compatible with a local/retarded or otherwise explicitly causal initial-value structure, not merely circuit ordering.

M8. **Pairwise weak-field recovery** — reduces to the already validated weak-field pairwise branch with the same calibrated coupling.

M9. **Positive operational route** — defines a route from the nonlinear carrier dynamics and state to normalized positive operational evolution; generic future quantization is insufficient.

M10. **Selector power** — if fully instantiated, the principle must mathematically determine the connected response rather than leave NP1/CM1 freedom untouched.

## Decision rule

- Exactly one survivor: `PROSPECTIVE_PRINCIPLE_SELECTED_FOR_DERIVATION`.
- More than one survivor with no internally defined tie-break: `AMBIGUOUS_PRINCIPLE_SET`.
- Zero survivors: `NO_PRINCIPLE_SELECTED`.

A survivor is not yet a theory and does not authorize a `chi_ABC` computation. It authorizes only a derivation/falsification gate in which the nonlinear equations/source map/operational map are obtained and checked without connected-outcome tuning.

## Specific anti-bias tests

1. Remove the words "three-source" and "connected" from the candidate statement. The principle must still be physically motivated.
2. Replace the target observable by an unrelated nonlinear observable. The principle statement must remain unchanged.
3. Ask whether the candidate could equally select `lambda=0` if that followed from its dynamics. If not, reject as outcome-biased.
4. Ask whether two inequivalent nonlinear generators satisfying the same principle can be constructed while retaining all frozen inputs. If yes, M10 fails until the ambiguity is closed.
5. Any appeal to GR or another established theory counts only as an external convergence check, never as the selection rule.
